8:00 am · about me

A coordinator who started with travel

My first job was coordinating bike tours around Vietnam. There were always many bookings at once, tight schedules and guests who expected everything to run smoothly. I loved the moment a plan finally came together.

Ten years later I still do the same thing at heart, just in different industries: travel, textiles, construction and now IT infrastructure. Today I coordinate AI infrastructure and digital transformation projects at General Resources, part of Taiwan's MiTAC Information Technology group.

English and Chinese let me talk directly with our partners in Taiwan. In my free time I am studying for an MBA at UEH.
